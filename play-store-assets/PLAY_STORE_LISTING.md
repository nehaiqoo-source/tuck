## Full Description (Humanized, Play Store ready)
Most of us have dozens of open browser tabs and hundreds of screenshots sitting in our camera roll. We save them planning to come back later, and then never open them again.

Tuck fixes the digital graveyard with one simple daily habit.

Capture with zero sorting
Share any link, text note, or screenshot straight from the Android Share Sheet into Tuck. No folders to set up and no manual tagging.

Review three forgotten saves a day
Instead of giving you another endless list of 500 unread links, Tuck shows you three older items each day as simple cards. Each card has a short summary and one concrete next step so you can decide to Do it, Keep it, or Let it go.

Private on-device AI first, Cloud Gemini when you need it
Screenshots often hold private information like clinic receipts, personal chats, or bills. Tuck uses Firebase AI Logic Hybrid Inference to summarize and tag items on your device first (using Gemini Nano via Android AICore when supported), even offline. Private screenshot images never upload to cloud storage.

Connect the dots in Cloud
When you want to combine several saved links and screenshots into one plan, such as turning scattered travel saves into a weekend itinerary, tap "Connect in Cloud" to synthesize them with Cloud Gemini.

Works everywhere you save things
Syncs cleanly with Tuck Web (tucktabs.app), the Tuck Chrome Extension, and iOS. Open source under the Apache 2.0 license.
