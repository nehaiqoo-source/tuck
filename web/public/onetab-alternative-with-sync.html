A OneTab alternative with sync and on-device summaries

OneTab earned its reputation for one simple move: turning 50 open Chrome tabs into a single list so your laptop fan stops spinning. The catch shows up a month later. Your links sit only in local browser storage with no phone sync, and a flat list of 300 blue links gives you no clue which ones still matter.

What breaks in flat tab-dump extensions

If your browser profile resets or you switch computers, local-only tab lists can vanish. Even when they survive, a page title like "Reddit - Dive into anything" does not tell you what you meant to do with that link.

How Tuck fixes tab saving

The Tuck Chrome extension grabs the page title and text snippet, summarizes what actually matters right on your device, closes the tab, and syncs the card to your Tuck shelf across Web, Android, and iOS. Every day, Tuck shows three older saves so you actually clear them instead of building a second archive.
