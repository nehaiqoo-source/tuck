Fixing ADHD tab hoarding and the screenshot graveyard

If you have ADHD, saving a tab or snapping a screenshot gives your brain a small hit of relief: "Good, I will not forget this now." Ten seconds later you move on, and that screenshot joins 3,000 others in your camera roll while 75 browser tabs stay open for three weeks.

Why Notion and folder apps make it worse

Traditional note apps ask you to pick a database, choose a folder, and type tags while you are saving something. If capturing takes more than two taps, you skip it and go back to taking screenshots. Then once items are buried inside folders, out of sight means out of mind.

Three cards a day, zero manual sorting

Tuck removes the setup trap. You tuck a Chrome tab in one click or share a screenshot from your phone. On-device AI handles the title, summary, and tags automatically. Every day, Tuck puts just three older items in front of you with three clear choices: Do it now, Keep on shelf, or Let it go.
