Why Tuck takes a different approach

Most read-it-later apps give you a long vertical list. Tuck summarizes each saved tab, link, or screenshot on your device first using Gemini Nano or Apple Foundation Models, writes one concrete next step, and shows you just three older saves a day. You either act on an item, keep it on your shelf, or delete it.

Connect saved articles when you need a real plan

If you saved four travel guides or recipe links across different days, you can select them on your shelf and click "Connect dots in Cloud" to merge them into a single ordered checklist using Cloud Gemini.
